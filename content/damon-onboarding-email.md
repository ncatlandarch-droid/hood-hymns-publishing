Go to: https://cofs.lara.state.mi.us/corpweb/CorpSearch/CorpSearch.aspx
(If you're in NC instead, go to: https://www.sosnc.gov/online_services)

• Management: Member-Managed
• Purpose: Publishing, merchandise sales, and media production

Save your confirmation — you'll need it for the bank account.

✅ STEP 2: GET YOUR EIN (Free — 5 minutes)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Your EIN is like a Social Security number for your business.

Go to: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online

• Select "Limited Liability Company"
• Select "Started a new business"
• Enter your LLC info
• You'll get your EIN INSTANTLY on screen

⚠️ Do this on a weekday between 7am-10pm ET. Save/print the confirmation.

Never mix personal and business money. Open a free business account.

Go to: https://www.novo.co
Click "Open a Free Account" → Select "LLC"

You'll need:
• Your EIN (from Step 2)
• Your Articles of Organization (from Step 1)
• Your driver's license
• Your Social Security Number

Once it's open, transfer $100-200 from your personal account as startup capital.
Download the Novo mobile app.
